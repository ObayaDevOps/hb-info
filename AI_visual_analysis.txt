HUMBLE BEEING — VISUAL LAYOUT ANALYSIS (desktop / tablet / mobile)
Date: 2026-07-02
Method: Ran the dev server and drove headless Chrome through 9 pages at desktop
(1440px), tablet (768px), and mobile (390px) — plus extra passes at 1024/1280px,
with real scrolling so the whileInView fade-in animations fire, and with the
mobile drawer open. ~70 screenshots reviewed and cross-checked against source.

====================================================================
1. OUTRIGHT BREAKAGE (fix before any design polish)
====================================================================

1.1 Desktop nav is unusable at 1024px
    The floating pill (src/components/Navbar.js:44) overflows the viewport:
    the logo is pushed off the left edge and the Shop button is clipped off
    the right. Anyone on a small laptop or iPad-landscape can't reach Shop.
    Cause: 7 links x px:8 padding + logo + two buttons at w:auto.
    Fix: tighter padding at lg, whiteSpace="nowrap" on links (right now
    "Our Story"/"Our Process" wrap to two lines even at 1440px), and don't
    show the pill until it actually fits (e.g. xl).

1.2 Chakra v2 props silently dropped after the v3 upgrade
    These are why several controls look half-rendered:
    - IconButton icon={...} (hb-home.js:97,121) -> carousel prev/next arrows
      render as EMPTY BLACK OVALS, on every slider, all viewports. In v3 the
      icon goes in children.
    - Button leftIcon={...} (Footer.js:198,214,230) -> donate-button icons
      never render.
    - maxW="container.xl" (hb-home.js, HeroCarousel.jsx:127) -> token doesn't
      exist in v3; verified in the DOM that these containers resolve to the
      1440px default, so sections meant to be 1280px-and-centered run
      edge-to-edge.
    - spacing on HStack/VStack (site-wide) -> ignored in v3 (it's gap now),
      so the intended spacing rhythm isn't what actually renders.
    - Contact page Switch (contact-and-connect.js:56) is missing
      Switch.HiddenInput and nests Thumb outside Control -> a stray unstyled
      checkbox sits next to the wholesale toggle.

1.3 Four broken images on Our Process
    The page still points at Cloudinary URLs that now return 401
    (HB_Single_Origin, HB_Pouring_Honey, HB_Beeswax, HB_Infused_Honeys,
    HB_Beekeeper_Field). Desktop shows four broken-image placeholders
    mid-page and the hero background is a flat brown gradient. Missed by the
    Cloudinary->Sanity migration.

1.4 Brand fonts aren't actually loading for body text
    Confirmed in computed styles: <body> renders in the generic system font
    stack. The next/font variables are applied to a <div> inside _app.js:44,
    but Chakra applies the body font token to <body> — where --font-poppins
    is empty. Home mostly hides this because every element hard-codes
    fontFamily='var(--font-hanken)', but all interior-page body copy
    (Our Story, Our Process, Impact, Wholesale, Contact) is in fallback
    system font — visibly a different, cheaper-looking typeface than the
    homepage. Move the variable classNames to <body> (via _document.js) or
    set the token fallbacks properly.

1.5 Shipped placeholders / broken chips
    - Impact page: literal text "Visual summary of aligned SDGs and outcomes
      (infographic placeholder)" plus a gray globe.svg
      (impact-and-sustainability.js:57-58).
    - Blog index: posts whose category slug doesn't match render an EMPTY
      BLACK CHIP (blog/index.js:143 — Badge renders even when the lookup
      returns undefined). React key / <option selected> warnings also fire.

====================================================================
2. DESKTOP (1440px)
====================================================================

- The homepage is ~10,000px tall and five of its sections are the same
  module: full-bleed photo, 95vh tall, text bottom-left, one pill CTA
  (Traceable / Mission / Family / Recipes / Quality Assured). By the third
  one it reads as the page repeating itself, and the products and
  testimonials get buried ~6 screens down. Recommend cutting to 2-3 and
  varying the shape — e.g. make Mission a split text/photo panel, Quality a
  stats-plus-logos band. Variety is what makes a long scroll feel
  intentional rather than endless.

- Fixed h="95vh" on those sections (hb-home.js:483,556,614,783,889) will
  clip content whenever copy grows or the viewport is short — use minH.

- Text-over-photo contrast is marginal in several places. The overlay is a
  0.1->0.75 top-to-bottom gradient, so anything not at the very bottom sits
  on barely-dimmed image ("Traceable." over the white bowl, "For the
  Foodies" body copy over white plates, mission text across a person's
  face). Add a stronger localized scrim behind the text block (or a solid
  panel) rather than darkening whole photos.

- The black product-slider container has minH='100vh' (hb-home.js:336) -> a
  big dead black band under the cards. Let content set the height.

- Testimonial cards are equal height with very unequal content, so two
  cards are ~60% empty with the attribution badge floating mid-air. Pin the
  badge to the card bottom (margin-top: auto) or let heights differ.

- Footer reserves minH='80vh' (Footer.js:63) with a 240px logo — roughly
  half of it is empty navy. Size to content; it currently reads as a
  rendering error.

- 12 hero slides at 6s each is a 72-second cycle nobody sees; several
  images are near-duplicates. 4-5 strong slides, and the 12 tiny dots stop
  crowding the marquee.

====================================================================
3. TABLET (768px)
====================================================================

- The product slider mis-renders: three clipped cards at staggered heights
  crammed left, ~400px of dead black below. There are also two copies of
  this whole section (desktop hb-home.js:334 and mobile :370) — consolidate
  into one Slider and fix the responsive breakpoints; duplicated sections is
  how these drift apart.

- 768-1023px gets the mobile hamburger even though there's room for more —
  fine as a choice, but the layout is otherwise a stretched phone view:
  statement headings at md:7xl are enormous at this width. Add intermediate
  type sizes for md.

====================================================================
4. MOBILE (390px)
====================================================================

- Overall the strongest viewport — hero, drawer, and footer stack sensibly.
  The drawer menu itself looks good.

- Slider arrows overlap the card image as empty black pills on both edges.
  On touch, drop arrows entirely (swipe + dots); also the slick dots under
  the mobile slider are dark-on-black, effectively invisible.

- Touch targets: the 8px hero dots are far below the ~44px minimum, and the
  drawer trigger is a raw <svg> (MobileDrawer.js:53) — not focusable, no
  aria-label, tiny hit area. Wrap it in an IconButton.

- 48px side padding inside statement sections (px: base 12) leaves ~294px
  for text — long paragraphs become very tall columns. px:6 is enough over
  a scrim.

- Four consecutive full-screen image sections feel like the same screen
  re-rendering as you scroll; the desktop-side cut to 2-3 helps mobile even
  more.

====================================================================
5. SYSTEM-LEVEL (why it feels inconsistent)
====================================================================

- Two design languages on one site. Home: Hanken type, pill buttons, cream
  cards, heavy art direction. Interior pages: system font, default-styled
  inputs, native selects, rectangular buttons ("Send Message", "Download
  our Impact Deck"), unstyled search box on Blog. Unify via theme recipes —
  one pill Button recipe, one Input recipe — instead of restyling per page.

- Hard-coded values everywhere: #f5cb81 / #FFF2D7 / #000819 and
  var(--font-hanken) are repeated dozens of times. Define them as semantic
  tokens (createSystem already exists in theme.js; note brand.red is
  currently cream). This is what makes the drift above inevitable.

- Repeated art: the garlic-jar-with-fried-chicken photo is the hero for
  both Our Story and Lab Tests; Contact's hero image is repeated inline
  immediately below itself. Each page should get a distinct hero.

- whileInView with initial opacity 0 on nearly everything means content is
  invisible until 30% in view — and with JS off (or for some crawlers) it's
  just invisible, since the opacity:0 is server-rendered inline. Reserve
  entrance animation for a few hero moments and keep body content visible
  by default.

====================================================================
6. WHAT'S WORKING
====================================================================

- The amber/cream/navy palette is distinctive.
- The blog post template is clean and readable.
- The lab-tests certificate cards are a nice pattern.
- The mobile drawer is solid.

Biggest wins per effort: items 1.1-1.4 (nav overflow, v3 prop fixes, dead
images, font wiring) — they're bugs, not redesigns.
