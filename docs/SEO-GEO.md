# Humble Beeing — SEO, GEO and search terms

Updated: 13 October 2026. Scope: improve existing pages without changing the layout, navigation or public URLs.

## Positioning and priorities

Humble Beeing supplies raw Ugandan honey and beeswax, working with smallholder beekeepers, alongside infused honey, candles and locally made gifts. Lead with origin, flavour, sourcing records and clear purchasing information.

1. Wholesale buyers: hotels, restaurants, specialty retailers, importers, cosmetics and candle makers.
2. Corporate gifting: clients, employees, hospitality and events in Uganda.
3. Premium consumers: honey, candles, artisan gifts and introduction ceremony gifts.

Uganda is the established market. UK and Swedish businesses are export prospects; do not imply local stock, delivery or export approval. Premium intent is expressed through provenance, craftsmanship, flavour and professional service, not by repeatedly calling products “the best”.

These are prioritized search candidates based on product fit, purchase intent and competitor research. No keyword volumes, difficulty scores, ranking positions or revenue forecasts have been measured. A keyword list does not guarantee rankings. Validate priorities with Search Console and qualified enquiry data.

## Evidence and wording rules

- Organic honey and organic beeswax are educational topics until an applicable current certificate is documented. Record the certifier, operator, product scope and validity before making a commercial certification claim.
- The public UNBS chemistry and microbiology reports cover sample L/5087/2025MC, tested in October 2025. They do not establish organic certification, UNBS product certification, testing of every batch, or pesticide, antibiotic and adulteration results.
- Explain what origin and harvest records can be supplied for the actual order. Do not invent batch reports, beekeeper counts, capacity, quantified farmer premiums or certifications to improve search copy.
- Keep ambitions visibly labelled as ambitions. Do not turn targets into achieved production or farmer numbers.
- Distinguish raw handling, floral origin and organic certification. Use “cold-pressed”, “unheated”, “unpasteurised” or a temperature limit only where confirmed for that product or process.
- Beeswax specifications and intended use require product-specific evidence. Do not add “food grade”, “cosmetic grade”, “certified organic”, “pesticide-free” or therapeutic promises without it.
- Confirm quantities, samples, packaging, lead times, stock and delivery in an enquiry when no published current terms exist.
- Describe what images show. Do not insert keywords into unrelated alt text or repeat exact phrases mechanically.

## SEO and GEO implementation

- Keep a clear search focus per page. Write distinct titles and descriptions, with related terms used naturally in existing copy. No keyword-density targets or meta-keywords tag.
- Product descriptions identify the actual category: honey, candles, cosmetics or gifts. Do not describe candles as raw honey.
- Link wholesale content to sourcing and published reports; link guides to current products and the appropriate enquiry page.
- Retain canonicals on `https://www.humble-beeing.com`, query-free. Keep sitemap URLs canonical and include the existing products, published guides and available recipes.
- Keep the visible business description and business structured data consistent. Article structured data identifies the canonical page and actual organization author; do not invent named experts.
- Match Product/Offer values to displayed products and prices. Do not manufacture reviews, ratings, availability or certification fields.
- Use visible, concise FAQ answers for buyer understanding. FAQ markup is not a promise of a Google rich result or AI citation.
- `public/llms.txt` is an optional factual summary. It is not a Google ranking lever. Keep it aligned with visible claims, products and source pages.
- Existing crawl access supports discoverability, but does not guarantee indexing or appearance in AI answers. Do not change crawler/training preferences as an SEO shortcut.

## Measurement and maintenance

- Baseline available Search Console data by country, landing page and query cluster; compare equivalent periods and note seasonality. Separate branded from non-branded queries.
- Prioritize qualified wholesale enquiries, sample requests and resulting orders. Treat email/WhatsApp clicks as contact intent, not confirmed enquiries or sales.
- GA is already configured in the application. This change does not add tracking or claim access to private analytics or Search Console data.
- Record monthly answers to the fixed buyer questions across selected AI search services: date, service, market, cited URLs and claim accuracy. Results are directional and variable.
- No numerical growth promise until a baseline exists. Do not infer that zero reported AI referrals means zero AI visibility.
- When the range changes, update product data, the associated guide, metadata and llms.txt together. Keep blog index summaries consistent with the linked articles.
- Use genuine publication/review dates. Update article modification dates only when the article or its metadata changes.
- After deployment, verify live canonicals, crawl access, sitemap coverage and representative URL indexing in Search Console where access is available.
